## Profile

Nine years in networks, all of them on the operator side rather than the enterprise side - a
carrier backbone, then a data centre fabric. The distinguishing feature of this work is that the
network is the product, so a change window is negotiated with customers rather than with an
internal change board, and a routing mistake is visible to people who have an SLA. I am hands-on:
I configure, I troubleshoot at two in the morning, and I write the automation that means I have
to do less of both.

## Incidents That Were Mine

In 2021 I applied a prefix-list change to the wrong peer group and blackholed traffic for about
40 customers for eleven minutes. The change itself was correct; the target was not, and there was
no pre-change validation because I had not built it yet. That incident is why the diff-based
validation described above exists, and I would rather present the tool with its cause attached.

I also spent most of 2019 resisting automation on the grounds that our estate was too varied for
it. It was not; I was protecting the part of my value that came from being the person who knew
the devices. I was wrong, and the engineer who pushed back on me was junior to me and correct.
