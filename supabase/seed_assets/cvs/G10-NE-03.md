## Experience

### Senior Network Engineer, Automation | VNG Corporation, Ho Chi Minh City | Sep 2021 - Present

- Own network automation and hybrid connectivity for a platform spanning two on-premises data
  centres and AWS - transit gateways, Direct Connect, and the routing between all of it.
- Rebuilt configuration management around NetBox as the source of truth. About 1,400 devices and
  interfaces modelled; configuration is rendered from it and pushed by Ansible, with a CI
  pipeline that lints, renders, diffs and requires an approval before push.
- Configuration drift went from something we discovered during incidents to something a nightly
  job reports. When we started, 31 per cent of devices differed from their intended state in some
  way. It sits under 2 per cent now, and most of the remainder is deliberate exceptions recorded
  in NetBox.
- Wrote the pre-change validation framework in Python - it takes a proposed change, renders the
  resulting configuration, and runs a set of assertions about reachability and policy against a
  network model before anything touches a device. Roughly 40 changes a month go through it.
- Designed and operate the Direct Connect and transit gateway topology - three VIFs, BGP with AWS,
  and route propagation policy that took two attempts to get right. The first design leaked
  on-premises routes into a VPC that should not have seen them.
- Built a self-service tool that lets platform teams request a firewall rule or a route through a
  merge request rather than a ticket. Median turnaround fell from about 3 days to under 4 hours.
- Still the escalation point for genuine network faults, still on call, and still the person who
  opens Wireshark when the application team insists it is the network.
