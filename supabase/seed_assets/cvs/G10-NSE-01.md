## Where I Have Got It Wrong

In 2020 I pushed a Panorama policy change to the wrong device group and dropped inter-branch
traffic for about 90 branches for seven minutes. The mistake was mine, at the console, on a
Tuesday afternoon. What made it seven minutes rather than an hour was a rollback I had rehearsed;
what made it happen at all was that I had refused to put device group selection behind a
second-person check because I found the check slow. It is now behind one.

I also spent about three years believing the rule base could not be cleaned because the owners
were unknowable. The cleanup above proved that wrong, and the tool that made it possible - hit
counters - had been available the entire time. It was not a technical obstacle; it was that
nobody wanted to own the risk of deleting a rule.
