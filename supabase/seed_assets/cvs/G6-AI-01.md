# Truong Quoc Dung

Cau Giay, Ha Noi | +84 986 104 337 | dung.truongquoc@gmail.com
scholar.google.com/citations?user=tqdung | github.com/tqdung | tqdung.github.io

## Research Interests

Representation learning for low-resource languages, with a continuing interest in evaluation:
how we decide that a Vietnamese language model is good, and why the standard translated
benchmarks answer a different question than the one we ask of them. Secondary interest in
efficient inference, arrived at from necessity rather than taste.

## Experience

### Research Scientist | VinAI Research, Ha Noi | Nov 2021 - Present
- Lead a group of four on Vietnamese language model pre-training and evaluation. Responsible for
  the research agenda, not only for my own output.
- Principal author of a 1.8B-parameter Vietnamese decoder model and its instruction-tuned
  variant, released openly in 2023. Roughly 90,000 downloads on Hugging Face to date and used by
  three external groups whose papers cite it.
- Built and released a Vietnamese evaluation suite of nine tasks with human-written rather than
  translated items. Inter-annotator agreement, per-task calibration study, and a documented
  contamination check against the pre-training corpus.
- Corpus construction: 240B tokens of Vietnamese text, deduplication at both document and
  near-duplicate level, quality filtering, and a licensing and takedown process negotiated with
  the legal team. Unglamorous and the most cited part of the work.
- Distillation study on the same family reduced inference cost by about 4x at a 3-point average
  drop on the suite; this is the version now used in two product teams.
- Supervise two research residents a year and sit on the internal paper review committee.
