## Statement

My research sits on the boundary between model design and the hardware the model must run on. I
came to AI from embedded systems, and the questions I find interesting are the ones a purely
algorithmic view of the field does not ask: what accuracy is achievable inside 512 KB of RAM,
what a quantisation error does to a control loop, and whether a published latency number was
measured on a board anybody actually deploys.

This is a genuinely mixed profile - roughly half of my six years is firmware and hardware work,
and reviewers have noted that my machine learning contribution is modest relative to the systems
contribution. That assessment is fair.

## Experience

### Researcher, Edge AI Group | Mekong Institute of Applied Technology, Can Tho | Mar 2023 - Present
- Applied research on TinyML for agricultural monitoring, funded by a provincial science
  programme and one industry partner.
- Designed and published a pest-detection model for rice fields running on an ESP32-S3 with a
  camera module: 4-class classification, INT8 quantised, 340 KB flash footprint, 1.1 seconds per
  inference at 240 MHz. Field-tested at eleven sites in Hau Giang and An Giang over two crop
  cycles.
- Field accuracy was 71% against 88% in the laboratory. The gap is the actual research result -
  it is caused by lens condensation and by the fact that our training images were captured in
  daylight by a person holding the camera steady. We have published this negative finding.
- Built the acoustic sensing prototype for pump-failure prediction in irrigation stations. Small
  spectrogram CNN on a Cortex-M4F. Accuracy is adequate; the deployment blocker is power budget,
  not accuracy, and I now design for milliwatts first.
- Principal investigator on a 900 million VND provincial grant, 2024-2026. Manage the budget, two
  research assistants and the reporting. Co-supervise four undergraduate theses a year at Can Tho
  University.

### Embedded Software Engineer | Terra Motors Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh City | Aug 2020 - Feb 2023
- Firmware for the battery management system of an electric two-wheeler: C on STM32, CAN bus,
  state-of-charge estimation.
- Replaced the coulomb-counting SoC estimator with an extended Kalman filter, then with a small
  neural estimator trained on cell test-bench data. The neural version was more accurate in the
  laboratory and did not ship - we could not certify its behaviour outside the training envelope
  and the safety review said no. I still think the review was right.
- Built the cell-data logging and test-bench automation that made the above possible: about 2,800
  charge-discharge cycles across 40 cells, and the dataset is still in use.
- Hardware bring-up on two board revisions, schematic review, oscilloscope-level debugging.
