## Experience

### Computer Vision Engineer | VinBrain, Ha Noi | Jan 2022 - Present
- Chest radiograph interpretation: multi-label abnormality classification and localisation, part
  of a product deployed in Vietnamese hospitals.
- Own the tuberculosis-suspicion model. AUC 0.94 on the internal test set of 12,000 studies, and
  0.89 on an external set from three hospitals that contributed no training data - the second
  number is the one I quote, and the drop is mostly scanner and exposure differences.
- Designed the reader study that supports the clinical claim: three radiologists, blinded, 600
  studies, arbitration protocol for disagreement, sample size calculated in advance rather than
  after. Sensitivity non-inferior to the average reader; specificity slightly worse and reported
  as such.
- Built the DICOM ingestion and de-identification pipeline. Pixel-level burned-in text removal,
  private-tag scrubbing, and a reversible pseudonymisation held separately from the image store.
  This is my hospital-IT experience doing the work.
- Subgroup analysis is standard in my evaluations now: by sex, age band, scanner manufacturer and
  contributing site. We found and fixed a real performance gap on portable radiographs, which are
  disproportionately taken of the sickest patients.
- Contributed to the regulatory technical file for a Ministry of Health medical-device
  registration - intended use statement, risk analysis, and the verification and validation
  evidence. Two rounds of questions, both answered.
- Mentor one junior engineer and the two annotation coordinators.
