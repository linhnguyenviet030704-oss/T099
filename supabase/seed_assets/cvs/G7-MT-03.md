## About

I test software for a living and I got here sideways. Two years on a customer support desk,
then two years as a business analyst, and for the last two and a half years I have been the
tester on a squad - which in a company of this size means I am also the person who writes the
acceptance criteria, because whoever writes them ends up testing them anyway.

The support years are the reason I am useful. I have read several thousand angry messages from
people who could not complete a payment, and it changed the order in which I test things. I
start where the money is and where the user is confused, not at the top of the requirement
document.
