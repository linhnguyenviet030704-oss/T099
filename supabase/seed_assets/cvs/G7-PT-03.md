Son Tra, Da Nang | +84 918 604 273 | khang.vodinh@gmail.com

## Profile

Performance testing is what I do; reliability engineering is where I learned to do it. Three
years on an SRE rota taught me that a load test result and a production incident are the same
document written at different times, and I have run performance work that way ever since -
tied to SLOs the team already agreed, measured with the same telemetry that pages them at
night, and validated by breaking things on purpose rather than only by adding users.

## Experience

  second.
