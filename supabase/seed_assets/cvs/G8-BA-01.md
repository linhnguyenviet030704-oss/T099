## Where I Have Been Wrong

The 2022 payment reversal incident above is the clearest. I had specified the timeout behaviour
for the gateway but not for the case where the gateway responded successfully after our own
timeout had already fired. It was a gap in my thinking about time, not about payments, and it is
why I now write specifications for concurrent and late events explicitly.

Less dramatically: for about three years I wrote long specifications because length felt like
diligence. A developer at TPBank told me he read the first two pages and the tables. He was right,
and the specifications I write now are roughly half the length with twice as many tables.
