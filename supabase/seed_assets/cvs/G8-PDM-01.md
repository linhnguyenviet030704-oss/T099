## Profile

Nine years in product, six of them in consumer fintech in Vietnam. I own outcomes rather than
delivery dates - the distinction matters and I will defend it, because the market here uses
"Product Manager" and "Project Manager" almost interchangeably and the two jobs fail in
different ways. My last three years were spent on lending inside a wallet, which is the part of
fintech where product decisions have a credit loss attached to them and you find out whether you
were right about twelve months later.

## Experience

### Senior Product Manager, Credit | MoMo (M_Service), Ho Chi Minh City | Jan 2022 - Present

- Own the consumer instalment lending line inside the wallet - a product built with two bank
  partners where we handle acquisition, journey and servicing, and the partner holds the book.
  Two squads, 14 people, plus a shared risk analytics team I do not control.
- Took approval-to-disbursement from a median of 40 hours to under 9 minutes for repeat
  borrowers, by moving the partner's manual review to a policy engine for the segment where 18
  months of our own repayment history already existed. The hard part was not the engineering; it
  was assembling the evidence that persuaded a bank credit committee to delegate the decision.
- Grew active loan accounts from roughly 90,000 to 410,000 over two years while first-payment
  default fell from 4.1 per cent to 2.6 per cent. Most of that improvement is the risk team's
  model, not mine. My contribution was killing an acquisition channel that was converting well
  and defaulting badly, over the objection of a growth target I owned.
- Rebuilt the repayment reminder flow after discovering that 30 per cent of late payers in a
  sample of 200 calls were late because they had not understood the due date, not because they
  lacked money. Changing copy and reminder timing moved 30-day delinquency about 70 basis points -
  the cheapest win I have ever shipped and one no roadmap process would have surfaced.
- Own the regulatory conversation with our legal and compliance team on Circular 06 and the SBV
  rules on electronic lending. I am not a lawyer and do not pretend to be, but a product manager
  in this space who outsources the whole of compliance ships things that get pulled.
- Run discovery properly: I sit in on customer interviews myself, roughly six a month, and I have
  never once regretted the time. Our quarterly roadmap has a stated bet and a kill criterion per
  item, and we have actually killed three.

## A Bet That Did Not Work

In 2023 I pushed a merchant-financing product into discovery and then into build on the strength
of a market size and two enthusiastic pilot merchants. We spent about five months and shipped to
40 merchants before I accepted that our distribution advantage - consumer wallet users - had
nothing to do with the merchants who needed the credit. I recommended we stop. What I should have
done was test the distribution assumption in week two, and I did not because the size of the
prize made me incurious.
