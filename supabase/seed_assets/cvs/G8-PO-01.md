## Profile

Product owner on a life insurance platform - policy administration, underwriting workflow and the
agent portal. Nine years, six of them inside insurance. The distinction I would draw between this
job and business analysis, since the two titles are used interchangeably on most Vietnamese job
postings, is the authority to refuse: a BA documents what the business asks for, and I am the
person who tells an actuary that his request is going into the backlog behind two other things
and may never come out. I have made that call badly and well, and the difference is whether I
could explain the trade-off in the language of his objective rather than mine.

## Two Decisions I Regret

I approved a partial rollout of the new agent portal to 400 agents in 2022 without a rollback
that preserved their in-progress applications. About 60 applications were lost when we pulled it
back. The engineering team had flagged the gap; I judged the risk acceptable against the schedule
and I was wrong, and it was an agent-trust cost rather than a technical one.

Earlier, at FPT, I spent a year prioritising by stakeholder seniority rather than by value,
because the escalation cost of saying no to a director was high and the cost of a low-value
feature was invisible. It works and it is corrosive, and I only stopped when a new delivery
manager asked me to justify a quarter's roadmap and I could not.
