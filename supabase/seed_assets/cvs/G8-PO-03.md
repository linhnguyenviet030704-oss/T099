## About

Product owner for internal finance systems at a retail group - the ERP, the reconciliation
tooling around it, and the integrations into the store and e-commerce channels. I trained as an
accountant and spent my first two and a half years doing general ledger work, then three years
as an ERP consultant, and I moved to the product side in 2023. The accounting background is the
whole of my usefulness here and it is also why this CV does not look like a product CV.

## Experience

### Product Owner, Finance Systems | Nguyen Kim Trading JSC, Ho Chi Minh City | Feb 2023 - Present

- Own the backlog for the finance and supply chain systems squad - 9 people, a mix of internal
  developers and two contractors from our implementation partner. Stakeholders are the finance
  controller, the accounting team, treasury and store operations.
- Ran the requirements and cutover for the migration from a heavily customised SAP B1 estate to
  SAP S/4HANA Cloud. 14 months. My part was the fit-gap - 190 identified gaps reduced to 46
  genuine extensions by getting the accounting team to accept standard process where the
  customisation existed only because someone in 2017 preferred it.
- Month-end close went from 9 working days to 5. About two of those days came from the system;
  the rest came from resequencing who does what, which is not a product achievement and I will
  not present it as one.
- Rebuilt the three-way match exception handling for accounts payable. Exceptions requiring manual
  intervention fell from around 1,100 a month to 640, and more importantly the remaining ones now
  route to the person who can resolve them rather than to a shared mailbox.
- Wrote the requirements for e-invoice compliance under Decree 123 and Circular 78 across both
  the ERP and the point-of-sale integration. Tight legal deadline, no room to negotiate scope,
  which is the opposite of how I prefer to work.
- Say no to about a third of what arrives. Most refusals are requests for a report that already
  exists under another name.

sequence of internal capability improvements justified by cost and compliance, and if that is not
the role, this CV will disappoint.

rather than analytical, and when engineers disagree about an approach I take the one the tech lead

fair description of where I would add value and where I would not.
