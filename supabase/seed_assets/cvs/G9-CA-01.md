## Two Designs I Regret

I built the landing zone with a single shared transit gateway and no segmentation between
production and non-production at the network layer, because it was simpler and we were small. It
took eight months and a security review to unpick, during which non-production workloads could
reach production databases at the network level - not exploited, and not defensible.

I also introduced EKS about a year before the team was ready for it. For roughly six months I was
the escalation path for every cluster problem, which is exactly the failure mode I now say I
design against. What fixed it was not training; it was reducing the number of things that could
go wrong, by standardising the cluster setup and taking away the knobs.
