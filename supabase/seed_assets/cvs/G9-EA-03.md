# Mai Van Hoan

Dong Da, Ha Noi | +84 903 887 415 | hoan.maivan@gmail.com

## About

Enterprise architect whose route in was IT audit and data governance rather than engineering.
Seven years at a Big Four firm auditing other people's control environments, four running a data
governance function, four now in architecture. The consequence is that I am strong on the
questions an architecture function is usually weakest on - who owns this data, what is our
regulatory exposure, can we evidence this control - and weak on the ones it is usually strongest
on. Most of the vocabulary below is governance and audit, and that is an accurate picture.

## What I Am Not

I am not an application or infrastructure architect. I could not design your integration layer, I
have no meaningful cloud depth, and in an architecture review board I confine myself to data,
privacy and control questions because on the rest I would be guessing with a senior title.

Read as a whole, this CV is arguably a data governance CV with an architecture job title on top,
and I would not fight that reading hard. What I would say is that the two functions are converging
in banks here for a reason - Decree 13 and the SBV technology risk agenda have made data ownership
and evidenceable control into architecture decisions, and a function without someone who thinks
this way handles them badly.
